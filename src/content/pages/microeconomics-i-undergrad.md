## Lecture Notes

<strong>Note:</strong> These notes are prepared for students’ self studies. There might be, however, typos or mistakes in the notes and/or something that I only explain in lectures. Please do not take this as non-necessity of attending classes. (Of course self-study is very important,) attending classes is the best way to understand the materials.  
I also provide Japanese versions. Since the class is, of course, conveyed in English, you do not have to use Japanese ones. If you are intending to find a job in Japan, however, learning Japanese is important; in that case, taking a look at them might be beneficial to you (The notes are written in “semi-formal” Japanese: it is relatively formal, but not fully)
